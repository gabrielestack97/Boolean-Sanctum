## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a gentle, comforting visual philosophy rooted in woodland foraging, tactile warmth, and playful kawaii softness. Designed primarily for mobile-first experiences, the aesthetic evokes the rustic sensation of a woven basket lined with clean linen, morning dew on moss, and quiet forest discoveries.

The design movement blends warm organic tactile minimalism with soft-surfaced neomorphic-inspired comfort:
- **Emotional Resonance:** Safe, nurturing, restorative, cozy, and unhurried.
- **Visual Weight:** Light, buoyant floating cards over grounding warm-bark backdrops, emphasizing breathing room, plump touch targets, and pill-shaped structural containers.
- **Sensory Metaphors:** Toasted bread crusts, sun-baked clay, acorn caps, woven willow branches, and tender forest shoots.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, forest-floor sanctuary. Rather than stark tech-neutrals, surfaces leverage unbleached linens and warm mushroom-cream shades over deep earthy tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#6D5341` - Warm Bark):** The grounding anchor. Used for primary typography, structural icons, tactile active states, and grounding button containers.
- **Secondary (`#768E65` - Sprout Sage):** A tender green evoking new forest leaves, utilized for success indicators, active progress chips, and vitality badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97D64` - Wild Strawberry Peach):** A warm, soft accent providing delightful kawaii contrast, notifications, primary CTA highlights, and heart/favorite accents.
- **Neutral Base (`#F4ECE1` - Toasted Linen Cream):** Soft, organic surface background preventing harsh glare and inviting sustained, relaxed interaction.

### Supporting Accents
- **Sky Dew (`#A8C5DA`):** Soft muted sky blue for informational notes and secondary pill tags.
- **Honey Pollen (`#E8B86D`):** Rich, mellow yellow for warnings, ratings, and cozy ambient badges.
- **Canvas Tint (`#FFFBF5` to `#FAF5EE`):** Elevated inner card fills ensuring crisp contrast against the deeper `#6D5341` frame and neutral body text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends the soft, rounded openness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the organic, approachable legibility of **Nunito Sans**.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with sturdy weights (600 to 800). The round letterforms create a bouncy, optimistic rhythm without sacrificing hierarchy or structure.
- **Body & Longform:** Set in Nunito Sans. Its gently curved terminals eliminate harsh digital sharpness, maintaining effortless reading comfort across small handheld screens.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, utilizing generous tracking and clear uppercase or title-case balance for rapid scanning inside badges and pill shapes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a plush, fluid grid prioritizing fingertip comfort and generous edge buffers. 

### Grid & Composition
- **Mobile Grid (Primary):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Elements snap comfortably to thumb zones with persistent floating pill navigations.
- **Tablet / Large Formats:** 8-column layout expanding outer margins to `2rem` to preserve a centered, warm single-focus column reminiscent of an open journal.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an organic 4px/8px modular base. Compact micro-gaps (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) keep chips and labels tightly grouped, while section blocks utilize generous breathing rooms (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) to preserve a calm, uncluttered atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through soft, layered tactile warmth rather than sharp sterile drops.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Canvas (Base Level):** Warm unbleached parchment (`#F4ECE1`) or warm forest loam bark (`#6D5341`) depending on container context.
2. **Resting Layer (Surface 1):** Cream-filled cards (`#FFFBF5`) sitting with warm, diffuse ambient drop-shadows:
   - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.04)`
   - Border: 1.5px solid `rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.06)` for gentle physical definition.
3. **Floating & Interactive (Surface 2):** Floating action buttons, modal dialogs, and draggable sheets:
   - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.14), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.06)`
4. **Pressed / Depressed State:** Micro-squish transformation (`scale(0.97)`) accompanied by a reduced shadow radius, simulating soft bread dough or yielding moss.

## Shapes

The design language is strictly committed to pill-shaped and ultra-rounded forms (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Containers & Cards:** Radii range from `1.5rem` (24px) up to `2rem` (32px), creating pebble-like tactile shapes that feel friendly in hand.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Fields:** Fully circular/capsule pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for all action items, tags, and inputs.
- **Inner Nesting Rule:** When nesting elements (e.g., image cards inside pill containers), the child border radius scales down cleanly using the standard concentric formula (`Radius_inner = Radius_outer - Padding`) to maintain soft optical harmony.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Capsules
- **Primary Action:** Pill capsule (`h-12` or `h-14`), rich bark (`#6D5341`) background with cream text (`#FFFBF5`). Gentle active press state reduces scale to 96%.
- **Kawaii Highlight Button:** Strawberry Peach (`#D97D64`) fill with pure white label for celebratory or conversion triggers.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Soft cream surface (`#FFFBF5`) with 1.5px warm border (`rgba(109, 83, 65, 0.18)`) and `#6D5341` typography.

### Chips & Badges
- Miniature full-pill containers (`py-1 px-3.5`).
- Accented with tinted pastel fills: Sprout Sage (`rgba(118, 142, 101, 0.15)`), Sky Dew (`rgba(168, 197, 218, 0.2)`), or Honey Pollen (`rgba(232, 184, 109, 0.2)`). Text adopts the darker tone of the corresponding hue.

### Cards & Plump Tiles
- Backed in toasted white (`#FFFBF5`) over cream canvas.
- Rounded corners (`2rem` / 32px), generous internal padding (`space-lg` / 24px).
- Optional dashed or dotted secondary inner dividers in warm bark tones at 10% opacity, recalling rustic sewing patterns.

### Form Inputs
- Rounded pill containers (`rounded-full`) with a quiet background fill of `#FAF5EE`.
- Outlines remain invisible until focused, when they reveal a gentle 2px halo in Sprout Sage (`#768E65`) or Warm Bark (`#6D5341`).
- Icon prefix and suffix slots use round circular badge backdrops.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Plump rounded squiggles and pebble discs. Active states fill with Sage Green (`#768E65`) or Bark (`#6D5341`) housing a cream check icon.
- **Switches:** Thick capsule tracks (`h-8 w-14`) with an oversized pill knob (`h-6 w-6`) providing an undeniable tactile feel.

### Iconography
- Line-weight Material Symbols Rounded with thickened strokes (1.75px to 2.25px) and soft circular terminals to harmonize seamlessly with the typography and curved containers.
